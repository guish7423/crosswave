About CrossWave

Building the autonomous company stack — one product at a time.

打造自主运营公司的技术栈——一步一步来。

CrossWave was born from a simple observation: running a SaaS business involves dozens of repetitive but essential tasks — content creation, social posting, customer support, competitor monitoring, deployment management. These don't need human creativity; they need reliable execution.

We built a platform where 10 specialized AI agents handle these operations autonomously. The orchestrator plans daily work, the finance agent tracks revenue, the deployment agent manages infrastructure — all working together from a single dashboard.

Our first product, CrossBridge, is live and helping Chinese SaaS companies translate their content to global markets. Polsia Fork, our autonomous operations engine, powers the entire ecosystem.

CrossWave 源于一个简单的观察：运营 SaaS 业务包含大量重复但必要的工作—— 内容创作、社交媒体发布、客户支持、竞品监控、部署管理。这些不需要人类的创造力， 它们需要可靠的执行。我们构建了一个平台，让 10 个专业 AI 代理自主处理这些运营工作。
